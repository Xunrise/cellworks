# Discovery: lenses and lanes

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** confirmed by the product owner through a throwaway prototype on the built city (EPIC-009); built as
STORY-015, STORY-016, STORY-017 and STORY-052 to STORY-055 (EPIC-010).

## Summary

A lens decides which blocks and districts exist and where they stand; towers keep their shape and the colour of
their crate in every lens. Each lens arranges its districts in a shape of its own, so the view says what it shows
before a label is read: the imports lens is a staircase of what builds on what, the calls lens a network, the
co-change lens an orbit, the cells lens the bounded contexts joined by the context map's roads. Imports run as lanes
on the ground, calls arc through the air; both are quiet until a building is selected, and then only that building's
lanes stand out.

## How we got here

The prototype ran in a worktree off the city as built for EPIC-009 and was iterated with the product owner on the
running app, with cellworks itself as the project.

| Tried | Verdict |
|---|---|
| A hard cut between lenses | Rejected: towers fly to their new places instead |
| Lanes as straight lines | Rejected: lanes run like streets or pipes, at right angles |
| One pipe per import, all apart | Rejected: lanes on one route merge; between two districts they bundle into a highway, one lane per pair of files |
| Steady pulses on every lane in the overview | Rejected: too dominant, and lanes pulsing in step read as a ladder across the highways |
| Strong grey pipes | Rejected: faint, thin lanes |
| Sporadic fast traffic in the overview | Kept |
| Speeds in screen points | Rejected: traffic changed speed with the zoom |
| Traffic timed by the lane's position in a list | Rejected: the list's order changed every frame and the traffic jumped |
| Imports blocks as clusters of files | Rejected: long walls that say nothing; a block is one file, or the files of an import cycle |
| Districts named "mostly <crate>" | Rejected: named after what holds them together |
| Rows labelled "builds on rows behind" | Rejected: height says it |
| Rows stepping back on the ground | Rejected: reads as height, but the lanes ran flat: an optical illusion |
| See-through pillars; slabs on thin pillars | Rejected: steps are solid boxes |
| Steps stacked corner over corner | Rejected: a staircase, each step edge to edge beside the one below |
| Lanes leaving the structure, floating at a raised level or hidden under a step | Rejected: lanes walk down the steps |
| A tests district as the home of all tests | Rejected: tests hang under the district they call most |
| Coarser call districts | Rejected: the calls lens keeps its fine grain |
| "Mine" as the name of the user's arrangement | Renamed to custom |

## Confirmed behaviour

### Lenses

- Six lenses: folders (default), imports, calls, co-change, cells, custom. A lens bar offers them, each with a hover
  explanation; L cycles. A caption at the top left says what the lens arranges by and what a block and a district
  are; a banner names the lens for a second after a switch and fades.
- Switching lens: every tower lifts off, arcs over and lands in its new place, staggered, about 1.5 s; the new ground
  is there at once.
- Relationship lenses (imports, calls, co-change) cluster with modularity (ADR-0005). Calls: a block is functions
  that call each other, files dissolve. Co-change: files changed in the same commits, over the last 500 commits.
- Districts are named after what holds them: imports "around <most imported file>", calls "around <most called
  function>()", co-change "changes with <most co-changed file>".
- Tests stay in the cellar (U). In a relationship lens a test file hangs under the district whose code it calls
  most; tests that call no placed code form the tests district. Code the lens connects to nothing, and blocks linked
  to no other block, stand in the outskirts.
- Shapes: folders a spiral of dashed circles; imports a staircase of solid boxes; calls a force-directed network of
  hexagons; co-change an orbit of double rings around the most coupled district, with dashed orbit lines; cells
  membranes; custom solid circles.

### Imports lens

- A block is one file, or the files of an import cycle (a strongly connected set), labelled "cycle: <file> +N" and
  marked with a dashed orange outline and the words "import cycle".
- Within a district, blocks stand in rows by import depth: what imports nothing else in the district at the back.
- Between districts a staircase: the foundation on the ground; each level that imports the ones below stands edge
  to edge beside the previous step, alternately up-left and up-right on screen, one level higher. Districts of one
  level continue in the same direction. Levels follow the net import direction, so imports both ways do not lift
  both. Each raised step is a solid box down to its footprint.
- Import lanes walk the stairs: across a step to the edge it shares with the next, down or up that face, and on.

### Cells lens

- Offered only when the project documents bounded contexts in `docs/domain/contexts/`.
- A crate folder implements the context of the same name; a project's settings may say otherwise:
  `[projects."<path>".contexts] Pricing = ["crates/catalog"]`. Code of no context forms the "no context" district.
- The context map's relationships are roads from membrane to membrane, named by their type and pointing downstream.
  Related contexts are pulled together.

### Custom lens

- Starts as the folder city. Dragging a block moves it with its towers; dragging a district's open ground moves the
  district; dragging empty ground pans. A drag is hit where the button went down. A district grows to keep a block
  moved out of it.
- Kept per project in Cellworks' own data directory and restored when the project opens (ADR-0006). No file of the
  project changes.

### Lanes

- Import lanes run on the ground on a 24-point street grid; within a district lanes on one grid line merge, wider
  the more imports they carry; between districts they bundle into highways, one lane per pair of files. Call lanes
  arc from the calling line to the top of the called tower.
- Imports, co-change: import lanes shown; calls: call lanes; folders, cells, custom: none. G and A toggle both.
- Every lane carries the colour of the building it starts from.
- Overview: thin, faint lanes; sporadic traffic, timed from the lane itself: on the ground 600 map units per second,
  every 0.6 to 3 s; in the air 2 s per crossing, every 2.5 to 11.5 s.
- A selected tower shows only its own lanes (what imports it, what its `use` lines import, its calls made and
  received); a selected block, folder or district shows everything in and out of it; nothing else is drawn. Lit
  lanes are traced in their colour with subtle pulses running downstream.
- Pointing at a lane reads what it carries; a click keeps it lit with its card; Cmd+click travels to the end
  farther from the click. A legend explains lanes, highways, colour and traffic.

## Settled while building

- When no block of a relationship lens links to another block, each linked block is a district of its own rather
  than the whole city standing in the outskirts (a small project's one cluster would otherwise vanish).
- In the custom lens a district's name, lying over the front of its district, moves the district when dragged.
- The lens bar and the lane switches are plain buttons, the current one marked by its fill.
